## – SQL Joins

### 📌 Overview

Today, I focused on SQL JOINs and how they are used to combine data from multiple related tables. Practical business scenarios were used to understand how JOINs help analyze customers, orders, employees, and departments.

### 📚 Topics Covered

* INNER JOIN
* LEFT JOIN
* RIGHT JOIN
* JOIN with WHERE
* JOIN with GROUP BY
* COUNT with JOIN
* SUM with JOIN
* Identifying unmatched records
* Customers without orders
* Orders without customers
* Employees without departments
* Departments without employees
* Combining results using UNION

### 💼 Practical Projects

#### 1. E-commerce Customer & Order Analysis

File: `Day9_Joins_Meesho.sql`

Created Customer and Orders tables and performed JOIN-based analysis including customer purchases, order counts, total spending, unmatched orders, and customers without purchases.

#### 2. Employee & Department Analysis

File: `Day9_Joins_Employee_Department.sql`

Created Employee and Department tables and used JOINs to analyze employee-department relationships, employees without departments, and departments without employees.

### 🎯 Learning Objective

The objective of Day 9 was to understand how SQL JOINs combine related data and how JOINs can be applied to real-world Data Analyst problems.

### 📊 Business Applications

SQL JOINs are commonly used for:

* Customer and sales analysis
* Order analysis
* Employee reporting
* Department analysis
* Finding missing or unmatched data
* Combining multiple business datasets
* Building reports from relational databases

* Scalar Subqueries

* AVG() with Subqueries
* Finding highest salary
* Finding lowest salary
* Finding employees above average salary
* Finding customers below average balance
* Finding customers above average balance
* Second highest value using a Subquery
* Subqueries with WHERE
* Subqueries with ALL
* Subqueries with ANY
* Correlated Subqueries

### 💼 Practical Projects

#### 1. Employee Salary Subqueries

File: `Day10_Subqueries_Employee.sql`

### 💼 Business Applications

Subqueries can be used for:

* Finding employees with above-average salaries
* Finding highest and lowest values
* Customer balance analysis
* Comparing customers against business averages

* Identifying records based on calculated benchmarks
* Performing advanced filtering and analytical queries
